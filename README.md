# System Dynamics Modeling of Ride-Hailing Markets

Academic project — University of Tehran, Spring 2026.

## Overview

This project examines how fluctuations in driver supply affect fares, driver earnings, and passenger demand in the Chicago ride-hailing market.

## Data

The study uses publicly available transportation and labor market data covering July 2022 to December 2025.

## Methodology

- Causal loop modeling
- Stock and flow modeling
- Historical behavior comparison
- Sensitivity analysis
- Policy simulation

## Main Findings

The simulation suggests that differences in supply-side and demand-side adjustment times can generate recurring fluctuations in driver participation and earnings.

Reducing platform commissions increases driver participation in the model, while a minimum fare policy primarily stabilizes earnings during low-fare periods.

## Limitations

The model is intended for exploring market behavior and policy scenarios rather than producing precise long-term forecasts.
